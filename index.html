LPG Leak Detection System with Automated Ventilation
Feb – Jun 2026 · RIT, Dept. of Electronics & Instrumentation
Selected for Pradarshana Techfest
Designed and built a hardware prototype using Arduino Uno and an MQ-6 gas sensor to detect LPG leaks in real time, triggering automated ventilation before gas reaches the Lower Explosive Limit. Engineered a servo motor-actuated 3D-printed louvered window mechanism with a torsion spring fail-safe — no human intervention required, even during power outages.
